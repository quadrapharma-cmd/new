# The Full Reference Catalogue — Beyond the WHO List

EDA's WHO list (`gmp-standards.md`) settles which **WHO** text governs. This file covers
everything else a GMP or quality document legitimately cites: PIC/S, EU GMP, ICH, the
pharmacopoeias, the data-integrity guidances, ISO and the industry standards.

**Last currency sweep: 29/09/2026.** Every "current" statement below carries the date it was
checked. Anything not re-checked on that date is marked *(not re-verified)*.

**Ranking rule (2026), applied every time:**

1. **Egyptian law, Chairman decrees, EDA guidelines** — binding
2. **WHO TRS** from EDA's adopted list **NP.CIP.001/2026 v04** — the governing GMP text
3. **PIC/S PE 009** — **EDA-adopted** since 01/2026 (**NP.CIP.003/2026 v02**)
4. **ICH Q1–Q14** — **EDA-adopted as binding technical references** (**NP.CIP.002 v3/2026**)
5. **The pharmacopoeia named in the registered dossier** where the question is a limit or method
6. **EudraLex Vol. 4, MHRA, FDA, ISPE, PDA, ASTM, ISO** — persuasive or supporting, never governing

Before 2026, PIC/S and ICH sat at "corroboration". They no longer do: both are adopted by the
EDA inspection administration. EU GMP was **not** adopted — it is textually harmonised with
PIC/S, so cite the PIC/S text and use EudraLex only to show the EU agrees.

A GMP document whose lead citation is ISO, FDA or ISPE has inverted the ladder.

## PIC/S — Pharmaceutical Inspection Co-operation Scheme

### The GMP Guide

**Current at PIC/S: PE 009-18, in force 24/09/2026** — *verified 29/09/2026 (PIC/S news, 23/09/2026).*
Adopted by the PIC/S Committee by written procedure on 22/09/2026. The only change is the
**revised Annex 19 (Reference and Retention Samples)**, principally **§9** on parallel-imported,
parallel-distributed and parallel-traded products; the note that §9 applies only where local law
covers such products is retained. Entry into force coincides with the EU Annex 19 revision
(published on EudraLex Vol. 4 on **23/06/2026**).

**Adopted by EDA: PE 009-17** — named in **NP.CIP.003/2026 v02 (11/06/2026)**. For a local
Egyptian manufacturer the -17/-18 difference is immaterial (Annex 19 §9 concerns parallel
trade). Cite as: *PIC/S PE 009 (adopted by EDA, NP.CIP.003/2026, listing PE 009-17; current PIC/S
revision PE 009-18, 24/09/2026)*.

**Annex 9 is the one most often missed** by sites making liquids. It covers homogeneity, the risk
of microbial growth in aqueous products, and the control of water used.

### PIC/S guidance documents (PI series) — VERIFIED against picscheme.org publications list, 29/09/2026

These are inspector-facing and therefore tell you how the inspection will be conducted. **They
are not covered by EDA's adoption notice** (NP.CIP.003/2026 adopts the GMP *Guide*, PE 009) —
cite them as persuasive, inspector-level interpretation.

**The one live change: PI 006-4 enters into force 01/10/2026** and supersedes **PI 006-3**
(25/09/2007). Published 30/07/2026. It is retitled *Recommendations on Qualification and
Validation*: one document instead of four (VMP, IQ/OQ, non-sterile PV, cleaning validation),
rebuilt on the 2015 Annex 15 with ICH Q9 throughout, and adds pre-qualification stages, PQ,
ongoing process verification, transport verification, packaging validation, utility
qualification and test-method validation. Any VMP or validation SOP that cites PI 006-3 is out of
date from 01/10/2026.

## EudraLex Volume 4 — EU GMP

**Not adopted by EDA.** Same chapter and annex numbering as PIC/S, because PIC/S and EMA are
bound by a co-operation agreement mandating harmonisation of the two guides. Where they differ,
it is usually because one has been revised and the other has not yet caught up.

**Parts:**
- **Part I** — Basic Requirements for Medicinal Products (Chapters 1–9)
- **Part II** — Basic Requirements for Active Substances used as Starting Materials (= ICH Q7)
- **Part III** — GMP-related documents: Site Master File explanatory notes, ICH Q9, ICH Q10,
  MRA batch certificate template, written-confirmation template for imported active substances
- **Annexes** 1–17, 19, 20, 21

**Status as at 29/09/2026:**

| Text | Status |
|---|---|
| **Annex 1** (sterile) | Revised, published 08/2022; in operation **25/08/2023**; §8.123 (lyophiliser sterilisation) from **25/08/2024** |
| **Annex 19** (reference & retention samples) | **Revised, published 23/06/2026** — *verified via PIC/S* |
| **Annex 21** (importation of medicinal products) | Published 16/02/2022, in operation **21/08/2022**. EU-only; no PIC/S equivalent. Relevant only to EU import/export chains |
| **Chapter 4** (documentation) — revision | **Draft.** Stakeholder consultation opened 07/07/2025, closed 07/10/2025. **Not final** as at 29/09/2026 |
| **Annex 11** (computerised systems) — revision | **Draft**, same consultation. Roughly four times the 2011 text; adds cloud, cybersecurity, AI/ML. **The 2011 Annex 11 is still the operative text** |
| **Annex 22** (artificial intelligence) — new | **Draft**, same consultation. Do not cite as a requirement |

Citing the 2025 drafts of Chapter 4, Annex 11 or Annex 22 as requirements is an error; citing
them as "direction of travel" in a gap analysis is legitimate if labelled as draft.

**Part III contains the EMA Site Master File explanatory notes** — read with WHO TRS 961 Annex 14
when drafting or revising an SMF. The WHO text is the one EDA adopted.

**Climatic zone for Egypt.** Egypt is generally treated as **Zone IVa (hot/humid)** or **IVb
(hot/very humid)** depending on the reference used — **30 °C ± 2 °C / 65 % RH** (IVa) or
**/ 75 % RH** (IVb). **The previous version of this file stated IVb without qualification; that
was not verified.** The storage condition registered in the dossier and EDA's General
Administration of Stability notices govern — check the *Submission Guide to the General
Administration of Stability* (v02, 09/2025) and *File Content of Stability Study Dossier* (v02,
08/2026) before stating a zone.

## Pharmacopoeias

**The registered dossier determines which pharmacopoeia governs a given product.** WHO TRS 1033
Annex 3 §4.1.1 states this explicitly for water: where specifications differ, the manufacturer
chooses *"in accordance with the related marketing authorization."* The same principle applies
generally.

| Pharmacopoeia | Current as at 29/09/2026 | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Egyptian Pharmacopoeia** | **5th Edition — electronic only** (egyptianpharmacopoeia.edaegypt.gov.eg; mobile app). ~2,500 monographs at launch, EDA reports >3,000 against a target of 3,400 | Legal force under Law 151/2019 Art. 19. Earlier editions were print. **Cite "Egyptian Pharmacopoeia, 5th ed."** and the access date — it is updated continuously |
| **Ph. Eur.** | **13th Edition.** Issue **13.1** (published 05/2026) in force **01/01/2027**; Issue 13.2 published 08/2026. **In force today: 12th Edition, Issues 12.1–12.3** (12.3 in force 01/07/2026) | Since the 12th Ed. (2025): **online only, annual edition of three issues** (x.1–x.3) replacing edition + 8 supplements. From 13.1, texts publish ≥ 9 months before implementation. Cite the **issue**, not just the edition |
| **BP** | **BP 2026** in force; **BP 2027** published 01/08/2026, **legally effective 01/01/2027** (incorporates Ph. Eur. 12.1–13.1; 18 new monographs, 132 amended) | Cite the year |
| **USP–NF** | **New six-issues-per-year model from 2026.** Official now: **USP–NF 2026 Issue 5** (official **01/10/2026** — Issue 4 until 30/09/2026). Issue 6 official 01/12/2026; USP–NF 2027 Issue 1 official 01/02/2027 | Cite **year + issue** and the official date. General chapters numbered < 1000 are enforceable, ≥ 1000 informational |
| **Ph. Int.** (WHO) | 12th Edition (2025), online | Referenced by the TRS annexes |

**Never substitute one pharmacopoeia's limit for another's.** Purified water conductivity,
TOC and microbial limits are not identical across BP, Ph. Eur. and USP, and a release
specification that does not match the registered one is a deviation from the marketing
authorization — not a conservative choice.

General chapters most often needed:
- Water monographs: Purified Water, Water for Injections
- Ph. Eur. 2.2.38 / USP <645> — conductivity
- Ph. Eur. 2.2.44 / USP <643> — total organic carbon
- Ph. Eur. 2.6.12 / 2.6.13, USP <61> / <62> — microbial enumeration and specified micro-organisms
- Ph. Eur. 5.1.4, USP <1111> — acceptance criteria for microbiological quality of non-sterile
  products
- USP <1207> — package integrity evaluation (framework for container closure integrity method
  selection and validation)
- USP <1112> — application of water activity determination to non-sterile products

## Currency checks to run before citing anything

1. **Is it on EDA's current adopted list?** WHO → NP.CIP.001/2026 **v04**; PIC/S → NP.CIP.003/2026;
   ICH → NP.CIP.002 v3/2026. The old **NP.CAO.004/2023 v2/2024** is superseded.
2. **Is the WHO text the one on v04?** 885 A5, 957 A1, 961 A6, 961 A7 and 1010 A8 (2015) are
   **not** — use 1052 A2/1060 A3, 1052 A4, 1044 A2, 1044 A4, 1010 A8 (2018).
3. **Which PIC/S revision?** EDA names **-17**; PIC/S current **-18 (24/09/2026)**, Annex 19 only.
4. **Is the PI document current?** PI 041-**1** (2021), not the 2016 draft; **PI 006-4 from
   01/10/2026**, not PI 006-3.
5. **Does the ICH code carry its revision?** Q9**(R1)**, Q2**(R2)** + Q14, Q3C**(R9)**, Q3D**(R2)**.
   The consolidated **Q1 is still a draft** until ICH announces Step 4.
6. **Is the EU text final?** Chapter 4, Annex 11 (2025) and Annex 22 are **drafts**; Annex 19
   revised 23/06/2026.
7. **Which pharmacopoeia and which issue?** Cite the one in the registration file, with its
   edition/issue: Egyptian Ph. 5th ed.; Ph. Eur. 12.x (13.1 from 01/01/2027); BP 2026 (2027 from
   01/01/2027); USP–NF 2026 Issue 5 (from 01/10/2026).
8. **Is the ISO standard withdrawn or replaced?** OHSAS 18001 withdrawn; **ISO 9001:2015 and
   ISO 14001:2015 replaced by the 2026 editions**; ISO 45001 is still 2018 (revision due 2027).

A document that passes all eight is citable. One that fails any of them reads as evidence that
the quality system is not maintained — exactly the inference an inspector draws from a stale
reference list.

## Procedure — new line in a licensed factory under IDA
1. **Layout review** (GL.CALI.001 §3-1): request on the designated form, signed by the chairman or
   authorised signatory, via the CALI portal + layout PDF → fee set by email per Decree 273/2021 →
   pay, upload receipt → committee meeting with a production AND an engineering representative →
   max 3 comment-fulfilment sessions → approval letter + stamped approved copy. NP.CALI.001: review
   10 working days; committee within 30 days.
2. **Layout content:** two hard copies in envelope files. Sheet 1 = raw material, intermediate,
   waste and personnel flows. Sheet 2 = air classification and room ΔP values. Key: company name,
   colour code, abbreviations, line activity. New line clearly located, **remaining licensed areas
   shaded**. Show LAFs, hatches, autoclaves, lyophilisers. No spelling errors.
3. **Add-line request to IDA** with the layout (§3-3.1) → IDA forwards to CALI → document review 10
   working days, missing documents within 15 → inspection date within 48 h → **joint EDA + IDA
   inspection within 30 working days** → if comments: official letter, fulfilment inspection within
   30 working days.
4. **Pay the per-line fee** and receive the amended Technical Licence of Operation within 15
   working days (NP.CALI.001). GL.CALI.001 §3-4 states **EGP 5,000 per production line** on receipt
   of the licence.
5. Licensing inspection summary published within 15 days after the licence (NP.CALI.001).
- Layout change without motive-power change (§3-3.2): form + layout before/after + bank-certified
  authorisation via portal → licensing inspection.
- Forms are "the designated form" on the CALI portal — **not published on the website**.
- Service list on the EDA fees page: items 595/596 (add production area with one / several
  non-sterile dosage forms to licensed areas), 599 (non-production area). Amounts not shown.

## After licensing
- Product registration needs the line on the factory licence (Variations guideline 6th ed.,
  12/2025; Decree 450/2023 regulatory guide v7).
- Update the SMF annexes 1, 6, 7 (TRS 961 A14).

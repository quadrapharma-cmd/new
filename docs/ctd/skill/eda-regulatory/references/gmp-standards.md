# GMP Standards — What EDA Actually Inspects Against

Registration and inspection are governed by different source trees. Answering a GMP
question from the registration guidelines, or from EU GMP alone, produces a defensible-
sounding answer that is not the one the inspector is holding.

**Last re-verified against the live EDA site: 29/09/2026.**

## The three adoption notices — the controlling layer

Since 2026 the Central Administration of Inspection on Pharmaceutical Institutions (**CIP**)
publishes **three** adoption notices, not one. Together they define the GMP reference base for
human, herbal, veterinary and disinfectant factories. All three are Notices to Applicant under
*Central Administration for Inspection of Pharmaceutical Establishments*.

| EDREX code | Title | Version / issue | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NP.CIP.001/2026** | Adopted List of WHO Norms and Standards for Medicinal Products, Year 2026 | **v04, 07/2026** | **VERIFIED — read in full 29/09/2026** |
| **NP.CIP.003/2026** | Adoption of the PIC/S Good Manufacturing Practices guidelines | **v02, issued 11/06/2026** (v01 1/2026) | **VERIFIED — read in full 29/09/2026** |
| **NP.CIP.002** | Adoption of the ICH Quality Guidelines (Q1–Q14) | **v3/2026, issued 05/2026** | **VERIFIED — read in full 29/09/2026** |

**Code change — trap.** The WHO list was previously **NP.CAO.004/2023 v2/2024** (Central
Administration of Operation). It is now **NP.CIP.001/2026 v04**. Any document still citing
NP.CAO.004 or "v2/2024" cites a superseded version. The old media URL (`/media/sifnsx10/...`)
may still resolve — do not treat that as currency.

### What the WHO list v04 says about its own status (§5)

- The adopted texts are **"the technical reference standard against which compliance is
  assessed"** — they complement, and do not supersede, Law 151/2019, its executive regulations
  and EDA guidelines. **On conflict, national legislation prevails.**
- An establishment **may adopt an alternative approach** if it demonstrates, on objective
  evidence and a **documented risk assessment**, an equivalent or higher level of assurance.
  **The burden of that demonstration rests with the establishment.** This is the clause to cite
  when defending a justified deviation from a WHO text.
- Scope (§3): EDA factory inspectors in planning, conduct, **reporting and classification** of
  GMP inspections; and manufacturers of human, herbal, veterinary medicinal products and
  disinfectants, licensed or applying.
- Basis (§4): texts adopted by the WHO ECSPP and published in the TRS. Language: English.
- §7 notes Egypt is lead country for the AMRH Technical Working Group on GMP and Inspection in
  North Africa — useful in advocacy, not in a file.

### What the PIC/S notice says

- EDA **"announces the adoption of the PIC/S GMP Guide Documents for Industry"** — Introduction,
  Part I, Part II and Annexes, listed as **PE 009-17**.
- Manufacturers are expected to follow and comply with the PIC/S GMP Guide, adopt appropriate
  international standards, and **validate any departure** to demonstrate equivalence or better.
- **"The EDA will closely monitor compliance with these guidelines during factory
  inspections."**
- Covers human, herbal, veterinary and disinfectant products.

**Consequence for the citation ladder.** PIC/S is no longer "corroboration only". Since
01/2026 it is an **EDA-adopted inspection reference**, alongside the WHO list. WHO TRS 986
Annex 2 remains the primary text for structure and for Egyptian documents; PIC/S PE 009 may now
be cited as an adopted standard in its own right, not merely as persuasion.

**Revision nuance.** EDA's notice names **PE 009-17**. PIC/S issued **PE 009-18** in force
**24/09/2026**; the only change is **Annex 19** (reference and retention samples), chiefly §9 on
parallel-imported/distributed products, which does not bite on a local manufacturer. Cite:
*"PIC/S PE 009, as adopted by EDA (NP.CIP.003/2026, v02, listing PE 009-17); current PIC/S
revision PE 009-18 (24/09/2026)."* Do not claim EDA has adopted -18 until the notice is revised.

### What the ICH notice says

- Formal adoption of the **full ICH Q-series, Q1 through Q14**, **"as binding technical
  references governing the development, manufacture, control, and lifecycle management of
  medicinal products"** in Egypt.
- Issued by the **inspection** administration, so ICH Q9(R1), Q10, Q7 etc. are now inspection
  references, not only dossier references.
- The notice itself lists Q3C(R9) and the Q1, Q3C(R10), Q3D(R3), Q3E work in progress. Drafts
  listed there (Q1 EWG Step 2, Q3E Step 2) are **not** final — cite them as drafts only.
- Minor internal inconsistency: the cover says v3/0, the purpose paragraph still says v2/0.
  Cite **v3/2026**.

## TRS 1033 Annex 4 (2021) — Data integrity — VERIFIED, read in full

Clause numbers below are quotable. Structure: 1 Introduction · 2 Scope · 3 Glossary ·
4 Data governance · 5 Quality risk management · 6 Management review · 7 Outsourcing ·
8 Training · 9 Data, transfer and processing · 10 Good documentation practices ·
11 Computerized systems · 12 Data review and approval · 13 CAPA · Appendix 1 Examples.

**§4.1** — "There should be a **written policy** on data integrity."

**§4.5** — Senior management provides the environment for a quality culture supporting open
reporting. *"Significant lapses in data integrity that may impact patient safety, product quality
or efficacy should be reported to the relevant medicine regulatory authorities."*

**§4.7** — Data governance must **define the segregation of duties** throughout the life cycle.

**§4.11** — A system for the **regular review of data for consistency with ALCOA+**, covering paper
records and electronic records in day-to-day work, audits and self-inspections.

**§4.15** — Includes *"using **controlled documents and forms** for recording GxP data"* and
*"defining access and privilege rights… ensuring segregation of duties."*

**§5.1** — **Data Integrity Risk Assessment (DIRA)** shall be carried out, documented and kept
current. Most sites do not have one; it is named as a requirement here.

**§10.4 — the operative paper-records clause.** Quote it in full when writing document control:
- control over the issuance and use of **loose paper sheets** at the time of recording
- **no use of pencil or erasers**
- **single-line cross-outs** with the identifiable person, date and reason — *"the paper
  equivalent to an electronic audit trail"*
- **no correction fluid** or anything obscuring the original record
- controlled issuance of **bound, paginated notebooks**
- **controlled issuance and reconciliation of sequentially numbered copies of blank forms**
  with authenticity controls
- maintaining a **signature and initial record** for traceability, defining the levels of signature
- archival by designated personnel in secure, controlled archives

**§11.8** — System administrators must have **no conflict of interest in the data**. Deletion and
configuration rights not assigned without justification and documented authorisation by another
responsible person.

**§11.9** — **No shared or generic logins** for systems generating, amending or storing GxP data.

**§11.11** — Audit trails enabled at installation, remain enabled, with **evidence of enabling** and
**periodic verification** that they remain enabled.

**§11.17 / §11.18** — Retention periods **defined in authorized procedures**; the manner of
destruction described in writing and **records of destruction maintained**.

**§12.3** — Routine review of data and metadata **includes audit trails**; frequency set by system
criticality.

**Appendix 1, Example 2 — form design.** *"Design formats to enable personnel to record or enter
the correct information contemporaneously. Provision should be made for entries such as… dates,
times (start and finish time, where appropriate), signatures, initials, results, **batch numbers
and equipment identification numbers**."*

**Appendix 1, Example 2 — blank sheets.** *"a numbered set of blank sheets issued which are
reconciled upon completion… bound paginated notebooks, stamped or formally issued by designated
personnel, allow for the detection of **unofficial notebooks and any gaps in notebook pages**."*

**Appendix 1, Example 7 — signature record.** *"A record of employees indicating their name,
signature, initials or other mark or seal used should be maintained to enable traceability."*

**Appendix 1, Example 7 — scribes.** Permitted only exceptionally, where recording would put the
product at risk; both persons identified; the performer countersigns; the process described in an
approved procedure. Quotes MHRA 2018 directly.

## Non-WHO references

- **PIC/S PE 009** — now **EDA-adopted** (NP.CIP.003/2026). Full chapter/annex map, revision
  history and PI series in `reference-catalogue.md`.
- **ICH Q1–Q14** — now **EDA-adopted as binding technical references** (NP.CIP.002 v3/2026).
  Current revision suffixes in `reference-catalogue.md`.
- **EudraLex Volume 4 (EU GMP)** — **not** adopted by EDA as such. Textually harmonised with
  PIC/S, so a PIC/S clause can usually be located by the same chapter/annex number, but cite the
  PIC/S text as the adopted one. Annex 19 revised 23/06/2026; Chapter 4, Annex 11 and new Annex 22
  are **drafts** (consultation closed 07/10/2025).
- **MHRA GxP Data Integrity Guidance and Definitions, Rev. 1, March 2018** — persuasive; cited
  by WHO TRS 1033 Annex 4 itself.
- **Pharmacopoeias** — Egyptian Pharmacopoeia 5th ed., BP, Ph. Eur., USP–NF, Ph. Int. The
  registered dossier determines which monograph governs. Editions in `reference-catalogue.md`.

**Citation ladder for a GMP document in Egypt (2026):**
1. Egyptian law, Chairman decrees, EDA guidelines (e.g. GSDP GL.CIP.005, managers GL.CALI.005)
2. **WHO TRS 986 Annex 2** + the topic annex from **NP.CIP.001/2026 v04**
3. **PIC/S PE 009** chapter/annex, as adopted by **NP.CIP.003/2026**
4. **ICH** (adopted, NP.CIP.002) where the question is scientific or lifecycle
5. The pharmacopoeia named in the registration file
6. EU GMP, MHRA, FDA, ISPE, PDA, ASTM, ISO — persuasive or supporting only

## The inspection and licensing administrations

Institutions (CIP)** → **General Administration for Factories Inspection** → *Administration of
Inspection of Pharmaceutical Factories for Human, Herbal, Veterinary and Disinfectants*
(contacts on the notices: Local Industry Development Unit `DFI.Devunit@edaegypt.gov.eg`; DFI Audit

(**CALI — Pharmaceutical Institutions Licensing** → **General Administration of Factories
Licensing**). A licence amendment does not answer an inspection finding, and vice versa.

## Other adopted annexes — key clauses VERIFIED (read 29/09/2026)

Source PDFs from who.int / cdn.who.int. Adoption and year confirmed on NP.CIP.001/2026 v04.

### TRS 996 Annex 4 (2016) — GMP inspection report (row 10)

The format EDA's inspectors write in, and therefore the format a CAPA reply should mirror.

- **§4.5** Every deficiency is **referenced to a GMP text, WHO guideline or MA commitment**; *"an
  observation that cannot be reasonably referenced should not be listed as a deficiency."* — the
  clause to invoke when a finding cites no requirement.
- **§4.6** Each non-compliance states **(R) requirement, (E) evidence, (D) deficiency**. Answer in
  the same structure.
- **§4.7** The report distinguishes a **defect in the system** (SOP inadequate/missing) from a
  **failure to follow it**. Diagnose which one before writing the CAPA — they need different fixes.
- **§4.8** Deficiencies sharing one system failure are grouped under that system heading.
- **§4.10** The report should **not propose specific solutions**; the CAPA is the site's design.
- **§4.11 Classification:** **Critical** — has produced, or may produce, a product harmful to the
  user. **Major** — non-critical, and (a) product may not comply with its MA, (b) major deviation
  from the GMP guide, (c) failure in batch-release procedures, (d) QA/QC head failing their duties,
  or (e) several "other" deficiencies that together amount to a major. **Other** — a departure from
  GMP not critical or major, or with insufficient information to classify higher.
- **§4.11.4–4.11.5** Classification is risk-based and product-dependent; **a deficiency repeated
  from a previous inspection may be raised to a higher class.**
- **§4.11.7 Compliance outcome:** "other" only → acceptable, CAPAs followed up at next routine
  inspection; other + a few majors (**fewer than 6**) → compliant after CAPA assessment, CAPAs must
  include implemented/planned actions, **timelines and documented evidence**, may be assessed on
  paper; critical or **six or more** majors → unacceptable, re-inspection normally required,
  enforcement as necessary. (The number six matches the six systems inspected.)
- **Appendix 1** model report: Part 1 general information (incl. licence number, key personnel,
  GMP guidelines used); Part 3 deficiencies; Part 5 references; Part 6 assessment of the company's
  response, final conclusion, **risk rating (L/M/H/C)** and next-inspection date.
- **Appendix 2** example frequency (months) by risk category × compliance rating (Good /
  Satisfactory / Basic): Low 48/36/24; Medium 36/24/18; High 30/20/15; Critical 24/18/12;
  unacceptable → case by case.
- Read with **PIC/S PI 040-1** (classification of GMP deficiencies).

### TRS 961 Annex 14 (2011) — Site master file (row 16)

- **§1.3** **25–30 pages plus appendices**; plans and schematics preferred to narrative; readable
  on A4.
- **§1.4** Part of the QMS: **edition number, effective date and review-by date**; each annex may
  carry its own effective date.
- **Body, nine chapters:** 1 General information (incl. valid manufacturing authorization, list of
  GMP inspections in the **last five years**, current GMP certificate) · 2 Quality management (incl.
  **release procedure and the qualifications of the authorized person**, supplier/contractor
  management, QRM, PQR) · 3 Personnel · 4 Premises and equipment · 5 Documentation · 6 Production
  · 7 Quality control · 8 Distribution, complaints, product defects and recalls · 9 Self-inspection.
- **Annexes 1–8:** 1 copy of the valid manufacturing authorization (for Egypt: the **technical
  operating licence**) · 2 dosage forms with INNs · 3 GMP certificate · 4 contract manufacturers
  and laboratories with supply-chain flowcharts · 5 organization charts · 6 production-area
  layouts with material/personnel flows **and room classification and pressure differentials** ·
  7 **schematic drawings of water systems** · 8 list of major production and laboratory equipment.

### TRS 1010 Annex 8 (2018) + TRS 1019 Annex 2 (2019) — HVAC, non-sterile (rows 20–21)

- **Part 1 (TRS 1010 A8)** contents: premises; design of HVAC systems and components; full fresh
  air vs recirculation; **air filtration, airflow direction and pressure differentials**;
  temperature and RH; dust, vapour and fume control; protection of the environment;
  commissioning; **qualification**; maintenance. Requires **detailed schematic diagrams** of air
  supply/return, **room pressure differentials and airflow directions**.
- **Part 2 (TRS 1019 A2, interpretation — non-binding examples):** recommended **minimum 5 Pa**
  differential for containment; *"pressure differentials of between 5 Pa and 20 Pa be
  considered"*; adjacent rooms' **acceptable ranges must not overlap** (e.g. 5–15 Pa next to
  15–30 Pa fails the cascade).
- **Classification of non-sterile areas.** TRS 1010 A8 §4.5 leaves the level to the manufacturer
  (product, process, susceptibility) and requires the state to be named (as-built / at-rest /
  operational); §4.7 exposed product = production cleanliness; §4.12 final change room at rest.
  **Current EDA practice per the user (03/11/2026): open non-sterile product rooms are Grade C
  (ISO 7 at rest).** No published EDA text found for this — get the written basis before citing.
- Cite Part 1 for the requirement, Part 2 for the number — and state the number as a WHO
  *recommendation*, not a limit, unless the site's own URS adopts it.

### TRS 1019 Annex 3 (2019) — Validation (row 26)

- **Appendices:** 1 HVAC (→ TRS 1010 A8) · 2 water systems · 3 **cleaning validation** · 4
  **analytical procedure validation** · 5 **computerized systems** · 6 **qualification** · 7
  **non-sterile process validation**. *(App. 2 cross-refers to TRS 970 Annex 2 on water, which is
  itself superseded by TRS 1033 Annex 3 — cite 1033 A3 for water.)*
- **§4.2** Qualification normally precedes validation. **§5.3** Predetermined protocols and
  documented reports. **§5.10** Scope and extent based on **QRM** (TRS 981 A2). **§5.11**
  Worst-case / challenge tests where necessary.
- **§7.1–7.2** A **validation master plan** is required, reviewed at regular intervals.
- **§8.4** Protocols approved by responsible persons **including the quality unit** before use.
- **§9.6** Report conclusion states success or failure and recommends monitoring and alert/action
  limits. **§9.8** QA approves where appropriate.
- **§10.5** Logical flow: premises → utilities → equipment → procedures/processes. **§10.6** Start
  with a **URS**. **§10.7** One stage completed before the next begins. **§10.8–10.9** IQ/OQ may
  suffice for simple items; **major equipment and critical utilities need URS, DQ, IQ, OQ and PQ**.
  **§10.14** FAT/SAT where appropriate. **§10.18** Calibrate control and measuring devices
  identified by risk assessment.
- **§11.1–11.4** Maintain the validated state by periodic review; **revalidation based on need
  and risk**; changes through change control; periodic revalidation where appropriate.
- Read with **PIC/S PI 006-4 (in force 01/10/2026)**.

### TRS 1052 Annex 4 (2024) — Pharmaceutical QC laboratories (row 28)

- Replaces **TRS 957 Annex 1 (2010)**; written to be consistent with **ISO/IEC 17025:2017**. New
  emphasis on **risk management, crisis management and business continuity** (post-COVID).
- Structure: management and infrastructure; resources (premises §5.2, equipment §5.3, reagents
  §5.4, **reference substances §5.5**); technical activities (sampling §6.1, method selection /
  validation / verification §6.3, testing, evaluation, **measurement uncertainty §6.7**, validity
  of results §6.8, **OOS §6.9**, reporting, nonconforming work, **retained samples §6.12**); safety.
  Appendix 1 lists equipment for a first-stage / medium QC lab.
- **OOS (§6.75–6.78):** checklist review by supervisor **with the analyst before any retest**;
  original preparations kept if stable; check procedures, raw data, calculations, equipment
  qualification/calibration and system suitability, reagents/reference standards, glassware. An
  **identified error invalidates the result** and the retest is by the **same analyst**; a result
  may be rejected **only if clearly due to an identified error**; if inconclusive, a confirmatory
  determination by **another trained analyst**.
- **Retained samples (§6.97–6.99):** enough for **at least two reanalyses**, kept **in original
  packaging**; disposal criteria defined.
- Read with **PIC/S PI 023-2** (QC laboratory aide-memoire).

### Decree 450/2023 — Unified registration rules for human medical products

**Status: VERIFIED. Read in full 05/08/2026.**

Signed 19/07/2023 by EDA Chairman Prof. Dr. Tamer Mohamed Essam. Published in *Al-Waqa'i Al-Misriya* No. 164 (Supplement A), 30/07/2023. Effective the day after publication; repeals all conflicting provisions. Six pages, seventeen articles.

**Article 4 — registration routes.** Routine registration in three Cases. Case 1: within the equivalents-basket number, CTD required. Case 2: accelerated registration — Route (a) imported products approved by **both** US FDA and EMA, registered within **1 month** of receiving a complete CTD; Route (b) approved by **either** FDA or EMA, **2 months**; Route (c) imported or locally manufactured, **6 months**. Case 3: exceeding the basket number, via routes for shortage-list products, rare production lines, newly licensed factories (10-year window), factories under construction, and products with at least 25% of output exported. Non-routine registration is available in emergency circumstances on a reasoned technical memorandum approved by the Chairman.

**Article 9 — validity and appeal.** Final registration notification valid **10 years** from Technical Committee approval. A safety, quality and efficacy report must be submitted **in the fifth year**, failing which circulation is suspended. On refusal, the company may petition for reconsideration within **60 working days** of notification; the petition must contain full technical justification and supporting documents, and must be decided within 60 working days. A refusal decision by the Technical Committee **must be reasoned** (مسبباً).

**Article 11 — ownership transfer.** Prohibited before final registration notification; before 3 years from circulation date for Case 1/2 local products for local circulation; before 5 years for Case 3 products. CAPP approval required in all cases.

**Article 12 — cancellation of the registration notification (إلغاء إخطار التسجيل).** Effected by decision of the **EDA Chairman**, on a **reasoned report** (تقرير مسبب) from the Head of CAPP, **after presentation to the Technical Committee for Drug Control**, in these cases only:

- (a) Local product not produced for local circulation, or imported product not imported, within **18 months** of the final registration notification — on a report from the Central Administration of Operations. Case 3 products excluded.
- (b) Not produced or imported before the expiry date of the last batch produced or imported — on a report from the Central Administration of Operations.
- (c) Failure to submit accelerated and long-term stability studies on the first three production batches within **5 years** of the final notification.
- (d) Non-compliance with the ownership-transfer rules in Article 11.
- (e) Circulation of the product would harm public health — on a technical memorandum supported by scientific evidence from the Head of CAPP, after presentation to the Technical Committee.

Products registered for export only, or for export and tenders under Case 1, are **not** subject to the (a) and (b) deadlines. Generics whose originator held an Egyptian patent are exempt from these deadlines until protection expires.

**Extension mechanism.** Where deadlines are exceeded the company may apply to the G.A. for Registration for an extension, stating reasons and the new period sought; the application goes to the Chairman with a detailed report covering the reasons for delay, the product's importance, and evidence of the company's seriousness. An extension of **6 months, renewable once**, may be granted on payment of the service fee and if one of the following holds: fewer than five equivalents of the same strength and dosage form actually marketed by other companies; the product is on the current shortage list; the product has locally circulating strengths from the same basket; or another case approved by the Technical Committee **by reasoned decision**.

**Article 13 — cancellation of the registration *request* (إلغاء طلب التسجيل).** Where the deadlines in the decree and its regulatory guide are exceeded, by decision of the **Head of CAPP**, based on reports from the relevant central administrations and after presentation to the Technical Committee.

Note the deliberate asymmetry: cancelling a *notification* (Art. 12) requires a Chairman decision; cancelling a *request* (Art. 13) requires only a CAPP decision. Both require Technical Committee presentation and a reasoned basis.

**Article 14 — transitional.** Prior registration decrees continue to apply to products submitted before this decree took effect, until their registration notification expires.

**Article 16.** CAPP must issue the Regulatory Guide to this decree within 15 working days, and update it as law, regulation and science develop.

## Central Administration of Drug Control (CADC)

### GL.CADC.002 — Guidelines for File Assessment for Pharmaceutical Products for Human Use

**Status: VERIFIED (v1/2021) — SUPERSEDED. v2 (05/2024) confirmed operative on the live CADC index 29/09/2026; v2 body not yet read clause-by-clause.**

- v1/2021: issue and effective date 4/2021. Read in full 05/08/2026.
- v2/2024: indexed only. **Confirm which is operative before citing.**

Structure: main text (scope, Group I/II rules, composition, methods) plus five annexes — Annex I physical analysis checklists by dosage form, Annex II in-house dissolution method development, Annex III chemical analysis, Annex IV microbiological analysis, Annex V file format for Groups 1 & 2.

Scope: finished pharmaceutical products for human use, local and imported, submitted to CADC before marketing authorization, for MA renewal/re-registration, and for post-approval changes.

**Clauses that decide cases (v1/2021):**

*Dissolution applicability by dosage form (Annex I).* The checklists are dosage-form specific, and this is where most dissolution disputes resolve:

- **Powders (§9, test 7)** — dissolution applies to powder reconstituted to form an oral *suspension* (unless otherwise justified), and to powder reconstituted to sustained ophthalmic or parenteral forms. A powder reconstituted to a **solution** is not in the applicability list.
- **Solutions (§10)** — the checklist contains **no dissolution test**. Tests listed are description, minimum fill, mass uniformity, pH, specific gravity/viscosity, particulate and foreign matter, uniformity of mass of delivered doses, uniformity of dose of oral drops, deliverable volume, container content for injection, and osmolality.
- **Locally acting APIs** — a footnote recurring across the capsules, granules, powders, suppositories, suspensions and tablets checklists states that where the API is locally acting and not systemically absorbed, the dissolution rate test may be omitted; for several forms it adds that disintegration time is sufficient.
- **Particle size in place of dissolution** — permitted where development studies show particle size is the primary factor influencing dissolution, with justification and defined size limits.
- **Disintegration substituting for dissolution** — permitted with manufacturer justification under ICH Q6A; not applicable to sublingual forms.

*When a comparative dissolution study is required (Annex II).* Framed for immediate-release solid oral dosage forms, by method reference:

| Method reference | Comparative study required? |
|---|---|
| USP or BP specific monograph | Not required |
| FDA dissolution methods database | Profile with the reference in the most suitable medium only |
| Highly soluble drugs (BCS I/III) meeting FDA Aug 2018 criteria, Q=80% in 30 min | Not required |
| In-house method, Q=75% in 45 min or less | Profile with the reference in the most suitable medium only |
| In-house method, Q<75% at 45 min or slower | **Full comparative study in pH 1.2, 4.5 and 6.8 plus the most suitable medium** |

Similarity criteria: f1 up to 15, f2 above 50. Only one measurement counted after 85% dissolution; %CV not more than 20% at early points and not more than 10% at later points.

*Assay acceptance limits (Annex III).* API 90–110% of label claim; preservative 80–120%; antioxidant 50–120%. Wider deviation accepted only if justified by a specific FPP monograph or approved stability specifications. Narrower limits always accepted.

*Reliance.* Imported FPPs approved by a country on the Technical Committee for Drug Control's reference-country list may be considered for the reliance pathway at the applicant's discretion, via pre-submission assessment before samples are submitted.

*Group I vs Group II.* Group I = products with existing MA submitted to Post Approval Control for testing other than variations. Group II = products submitted for MA, renewal/re-registration or post-approval variations, plus specific post-approval variations (API supplier change, manufacturing site change or addition, scale-up).

### WHO TRS 986 Annex 2 (2014) — GMP for pharmaceutical products: main principles

- **Status: VERIFIED** — fetched and read in full (60 pages)
- Adopted by EDA per NP.CIP.001/2026 v04 (07/2026). **The primary GMP guideline in Egypt.**
- Revises **WHO TRS 961 Annex 3 (2011)**.
- URL: https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/trs986-annex2

Its own General Considerations state it is used *"as a basis for the inspection of manufacturing
facilities"* and as training material for government medicines inspectors. Every other adopted
annex is supplementary to it. Footnote: *"The word 'should' in the text means a strong
recommendation."*

Full clause-level treatment in `references/gmp-core.md`. The clauses cited most often:
**§1.6** management review at least annually; **§1.10** product quality review of all products
annually with twelve listed items and **no minimum batch count**; **§4.2** validation master plan;
**§4.5** qualification is not a one-off, annual review; **§8.2** fifteen self-inspection items;
**§8.4** at least annually; **§9.3** no gaps or unexplained overlaps, organization chart required;
**§9.6** heads of production and quality unit **independent of each other**; **§9.7** acceptable
scientific education **lists chemistry first**, subject to national legislation; **§9.14** the ten
checks the authorized person must make before release, including **(c) processes validated**;
**§9.15** release **may be delegated** to a named qualified person under an approved procedure,
normally QA by batch review; **§13.13** current drawings of critical equipment; **§15.5** a system
to prevent inadvertent use of superseded versions; **§15.8** records retained **at least one year
after the expiry date**; **§15.31** nine mandatory SOP subjects; **§15.35** an SOP for each
instrument placed near it; **§15.40–15.41** numbers not reused and allocation **immediately
recorded in a register**; **§16.23** calibration label with date done and due, plus daily
instrument check; **§16.34** reconciliation discrepancies recorded **before** release;
**§17.2** independence of QC from production is fundamental.
